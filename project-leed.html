Sustainable Excellence

The World Bank Country Office, Sierra Leone, proudly holds the internationally esteemed LEED Gold Certification, reflecting an exceptional commitment to environmental responsibility, energy efficiency, and occupant well-being.

This prestigious certification, awarded by the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC) and verified by Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI), recognises buildings that excel in sustainable design, construction quality, operational performance, and long-term environmental impact.

Energy Efficiency & Water Conservation

The facility integrates advanced HVAC systems, energy-efficient lighting, and smart building controls — significantly reducing energy consumption while improving operational efficiency.

High-performance fixtures, efficient water systems, and rainwater harvesting contribute to responsible water management and reduced consumption.

Indoor Environmental Quality & Sustainable Materials

Enhanced ventilation, abundant natural daylight, and low-emitting materials ensure a healthy, productive, and comfortable workspace for all occupants.

Recycled, locally sourced, and environmentally responsible materials were incorporated throughout the reconstruction process.

Waste Reduction & Location

Comprehensive waste-management strategies, including recycling and responsible construction waste disposal, help minimise the building's environmental footprint.

The building's strategic urban location allows easy access to public transportation, walkable networks, and bicycle facilities — encouraging sustainable commuting.

In Conclusion

Achieving LEED Gold Certification for the World Bank Country Office, Sierra Leone underscores JYGA's dedication to creating built environments that reduce environmental impact while supporting wellness, productivity, and long-term value. This milestone reflects the practice's continuing leadership in sustainable, future-forward architectural design across West Africa.

Arc. Manilius Garber
Managing Partner, JYGA · President, SLIA
