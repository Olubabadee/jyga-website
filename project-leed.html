About The Project

Sustainable Excellence

The World Bank Country Office, Sierra Leone, proudly holds the internationally esteemed LEED Gold Certification, reflecting an exceptional commitment to environmental responsibility, energy efficiency, and occupant well-being. This prestigious certification, awarded by the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC) and verified by Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI), recognises buildings that excel in sustainable design, construction quality, operational performance, and long-term environmental impact.

  • Energy-Efficient Building Systems
  • High Operational Efficiency
  • Improved Indoor Environmental Quality

Performance

Energy and Water Conservation

The facility integrates advanced HVAC systems, energy-efficient lighting, and smart building controls — significantly reducing energy consumption while improving operational efficiency.

High-performance fixtures, efficient water systems, and rainwater harvesting contribute to responsible water management and reduced consumption.

01

Indoor Environmental Quality

Enhanced ventilation, abundant natural daylight, and low-emitting materials ensure a healthy, productive, and comfortable workspace for all occupants.

02

Sustainable Materials

Recycled, locally sourced, and environmentally responsible materials were incorporated throughout the reconstruction process.

03

Waste Reduction

Comprehensive waste-management strategies, including recycling and responsible construction waste disposal, help minimise environmental footprint.

The building's strategic urban location allows easy access to public transportation, walkable networks, and bicycle facilities — encouraging sustainable commuting.
