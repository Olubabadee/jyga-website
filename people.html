Managing Partner

Arc. Manilius Garber

President, Sierra Leone Institute of Architects (SLIA)

A highly respected, internationally qualified architect, Arc. Garber has delivered distinguished projects across Sierra Leone, Botswana, Liberia, Zimbabwe, Seychelles, and beyond.

His commitment to innovation, sustainability and design precision guided the transformation of the World Bank Country Office into a modern, high-performance facility now recognised with LEED Gold status.

Under his leadership, JYGA Architects continues to raise the bar for contemporary architecture, green building excellence, and climate-responsive design in West Africa.

“At JYGA, our mission remains simple: to build greener, smarter, and more resilient spaces for the future.”
